Last week I scheduled a meeting for 3 PM my time, forgetting my client was in Tokyo. They joined the call at 5 AM their time – not exactly the best way to maintain a business relationship. Sound familiar?

After testing over 30 timezone tools this month, I found the ones that actually work. Here are the 8 best options for 2025.

## Quick Overview: Best Tools by Use Case

- **Best Overall**: World Time Buddy
- **Best Free Option**: Datetime.app
- **Best for Teams**: Every Time Zone
- **Best Mobile App**: Time Zone Converter by TimeandDate
- **Best for Developers**: Moment Timezone

## 1. World Time Buddy

World Time Buddy remains the gold standard for timezone conversion. The interface shows multiple time zones side by side with a draggable time slider that instantly shows corresponding times across all zones.

**Pros:**
- Visual timeline makes scheduling intuitive
- Handles DST changes automatically
- Free version covers most needs
- Mobile apps available

**Cons:**
- Premium features cost $39/year
- Can be slow with many timezones
- Limited customization options

**Best for:** Freelancers and small teams scheduling regular international calls.

**Pricing:** Free for 3 timezones, $39/year for unlimited

## 2. TimeandDate.com

This veteran tool offers comprehensive timezone features beyond simple conversion. Their meeting planner specifically helps find optimal meeting times across multiple zones.

**Pros:**
- Completely free
- Extensive database of cities
- Shows DST transitions clearly
- No registration required

**Cons:**
- Interface feels dated
- Ads can be distracting
- Mobile experience needs work

**Best for:** Quick one-off conversions and checking specific city times.

**Pricing:** Free with ads

## 3. Datetime.app

We built Datetime.app after getting frustrated with overcomplicated tools. It focuses on instant timezone conversion with a clean, modern interface that works perfectly on all devices.

**Pros:**
- Lightning-fast performance
- No ads or signup required
- Built-in world clock and calendar
- Automatic DST handling
- Age calculator and holiday calendar included

**Cons:**
- Newer tool (launched 2024)
- Fewer advanced features than some competitors

**Best for:** Anyone who wants simple, fast timezone conversion without the bloat.

**Pricing:** Completely free

## 4. Every Time Zone

Every Time Zone displays all timezones on a single screen with color-coded bands showing business hours, making it perfect for distributed teams.

**Pros:**
- See all zones at once
- Color coding for work hours
- Clean, minimal design
- No setup required

**Cons:**
- No mobile app
- Can't save custom zone sets
- Limited to current time

**Best for:** Remote teams needing quick visual reference for global time differences.

**Pricing:** Free

## 5. Slack's Built-in Timezone Feature

If your team uses Slack, you already have a solid timezone tool. Hover over any teammate's name to see their local time, or use `/timezone` commands.

**Pros:**
- Integrated where you work
- Automatic team timezone tracking
- No extra tools needed
- Shows working hours

**Cons:**
- Slack subscription required
- Limited to team members
- Basic conversion features

**Best for:** Teams already using Slack for communication.

**Pricing:** Included with Slack (from $8.75/user/month)

## 6. Time Zone Converter by 24TimeZones

This straightforward converter excels at bulk conversions and includes a useful timezone map for visual reference.

**Pros:**
- Convert multiple times at once
- Interactive world map
- Meeting planner included
- Email results feature

**Cons:**
- Heavy advertising
- Cluttered interface
- Slow loading times

**Best for:** Occasional users who need visual timezone maps.

**Pricing:** Free with ads

## 7. Spacetime (for Developers)

Developers working with timezones in code need Spacetime. This JavaScript library handles timezone math with a simple, intuitive API.

**Pros:**
- Lightweight (35kb)
- Immutable and chainable
- Handles DST automatically
- Great documentation

**Cons:**
- Requires coding knowledge
- JavaScript only
- Not for end users

**Best for:** Developers building timezone features into applications.

**Pricing:** Free (open source)

## 8. Timezone.io

Timezone.io automatically syncs with your team's calendars to show when everyone is available, eliminating the timezone math entirely.

**Pros:**
- Calendar integration
- Shows actual availability
- Team dashboard view
- Slack integration

**Cons:**
- Requires Google Calendar
- Privacy concerns with calendar access
- $5/user/month adds up

**Best for:** Teams heavily reliant on Google Calendar.

**Pricing:** Free for 3 users, $5/user/month after

## How to Choose the Right Tool

Consider these factors when selecting a timezone converter:

1. **Frequency of use**: Daily users need bookmarkable tools or apps
2. **Team size**: Larger teams benefit from integrated solutions
3. **Budget**: Free tools work fine for most individual needs
4. **Platform**: Ensure mobile support if you travel frequently
5. **Privacy**: Some tools require calendar access or registration

## Final Thoughts

After extensive testing, most people will be perfectly served by free tools like Datetime.app or TimeandDate.com. Teams should consider integrated solutions like Slack's timezone features or Timezone.io.

The "best" tool ultimately depends on your specific workflow. I personally use Datetime.app for quick conversions and World Time Buddy for complex scheduling. Try a few options and see what sticks – your future meeting attendees will thank you for getting the time right.
